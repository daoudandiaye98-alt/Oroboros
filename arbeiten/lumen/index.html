Questions

Everything people ask before booking.

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe it as light scratching. We apply numbing cream before and during the session, so the majority rate discomfort at two out of ten or lower.

How long does it last?

Usually 12 to 24 months, depending on skin type, sun exposure and skincare. A colour refresh keeps the shape crisp for years.

What does healing look like?

Brows look darker for the first week, then lighten by roughly 30%. Avoid water, sweat and makeup on the area for seven days — we give you a written aftercare plan.

Who isn't a candidate?

We don't treat during pregnancy or breastfeeding, with active skin conditions on the brows, or within six months of isotretinoin. The consultation covers this before any booking.

How does financing work?

Choose 6 or 12 monthly payments at checkout. Approval takes about two minutes, with no interest and no fees if you pay on schedule.

Is the perfecting session really included?

Yes. Every microblading, powder and combo treatment includes a perfecting session six to eight weeks later at no extra cost.
