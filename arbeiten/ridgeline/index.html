How it works

From damage to done in four steps.

01

FAQ

Questions before you call.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. You get the photos and a written report either way, and there's no obligation to use us.

Will my insurance cover it?

If the damage came from a storm or hail, usually yes, minus your deductible. We document everything and meet your adjuster so the claim is complete the first time.

Repair or replace?

If a repair gives you another ten good years, we'll say so. We show you the photos and let you decide.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes are done in one day, larger or steeper roofs in two. We protect your garden and clean up every nail.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, monthly payment options on replacements, with a decision in minutes.
