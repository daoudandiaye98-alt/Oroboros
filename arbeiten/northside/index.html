FAQ

Asked before every first visit.

What's included in the $99 first visit?

A full exam, digital x-rays, a professional clean and a written treatment plan with prices. If you need a deep clean instead, we tell you first and the $99 counts towards it.

I'm really nervous. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book. We plan a longer appointment, explain every step before it happens and offer sedation if you want it.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major insurers and bill them directly. Add your plan when you book and we confirm your coverage before you come in.

Can I pay in instalments?

Treatments over $300 can be split into interest-free monthly payments. The monthly price is shown next to every treatment.

What if it's an emergency?

We keep emergency slots open every day, including Saturdays. Book online or call, and we'll see you the same day.
