Questions

Go ahead and ask. Here are the answers.

Why does a website only cost €99 with you?

Because we build on a production line: a fixed process, proven building blocks, AI-assisted production and a fixed checklist. You pay for the result, not for meetings. €99 is our launch price.

Are there ongoing costs?

Not from us. No subscription, no maintenance contract. You keep paying for your domain with your provider as before. If you like, add website care for €39 a month — cancel at the end of any month.

Do you work with businesses outside Germany?

Yes. We work remotely and in English; everything happens by email and in your browser. Prices are in euros, and your card provider converts automatically.

Can I pay by invoice?

Yes. Write to info@oroboros-design.com and we will send you an invoice for bank transfer. Work starts when the payment arrives.

Does it really take 48 hours?

Yes, for the Standard website — the clock starts with payment and the completed questionnaire. If material is missing, we tell you on day one what it is, and the deadline moves accordingly. Premium takes five working days.

What do you need from me?

Ten minutes for the questionnaire: what you offer, how people reach you, opening hours, your logo and a few photos. If you have no good photos, we work with what is there and tell you honestly where a picture is missing.

Who owns the website?

You do. Completely, with no licence and no subscription. You get the files and the access. If you want to work with someone else afterwards, you can.

I already have a website. What now?

We build the new one next to it, you compare in your browser, and only after your approval does the domain move. Your old site stays untouched until then.

What if I don't like the draft?

That is what the revision rounds are for: one with Standard, two with Premium. Tell us specifically what does not fit — we rebuild it.
